## Cuts and landmark after 9d93b91

The first polish round stopped at 78/100 because every frame had to hold all 19 dancers, so the hero never filled the screen, the dusk sky stayed a thin band and the chapel never framed the scene. This round adds camera cuts and a readable goal.

- Cuts (`endingShot` in `endingPresentationView.ts`) are chosen from the accepted observation alone: phase, routine move and server ticks. Opening and toprock keep the high crane. Six-step is filmed from above the circle of guests, as a breaking cypher is. Spin, 720 and back flip use a ground-level camera inside the ring looking up at the hero against the sky, with the bell tower beside him. The one-claw freeze is a chest-high close-up from inside the ring. The finale holds a landmark cut (the crowd in front of the chapel and bell tower) for 210 ticks; the farewell then cranes the same camera up over four seconds until the crowd, the Frost shard, the torch path and the chapel line up.
- Stage frame: the guests stand still on two rings, so the mean of slots 1..n is the stage centre and slot 1 gives the hero's facing (`endingStageFrame`). The courtyard is anchored there and turned so the chapel stands behind the hero. Before, the scenery followed the hero's position every tick, so the whole village slid while his routine carried him up to 2.3 m.
- Hero cuts stand at a gap of the inner dancer ring (`endingRingStations`), inside it for the low and freeze cuts so the neighbouring guests are behind the lens. The station depends only on the fixed guests and the screen shape, so it holds still through a move; the aim (the middle of the posed hero) and the lens follow the pose. The pose hull comes from per-bone bind boxes of the skinned crab (`posedCorners`), because `Box3.setFromObject` keeps the first pose's skinned box. Tall screens use stations further out, because a 390 px wide frame cannot hold the spread spin from inside the ring.
- Framing is cached per accepted observation, rig count and aspect, so frames between observations do not recompute it.
- Wayfinding: two lamp posts now flank the chapel path (the post ring turned half a step), stepping stones lead from the court to the chapel door, banners stand at the door, a lantern hangs on the bell tower and warm light falls on the facade. Rocks and trees keep off the path and the house lots.
- Story: the shard of Frost's ice the hero brought home stands on a stone plinth where the path leaves the court, with a cool glow against the warm dusk. The freed Frost still dances with the village.
- Depth: a second row of houses fills the skyline around the chapel, pine woods climb hills from 60 m so the horizon is no longer a flat plain, and a second drift of petals and posies at each candelabrum fill the court floor between the guests and the candelabra.

QA contract change: wide cuts (crane, landmark, farewell) must hold every observed rig inside NDC x ±0.91, y ±0.61, as before. Close and low cuts are allowed only during their routine moves (close: six-step, one-claw freeze; low: spin, 720, back flip) and must hold the hero's exact posed silhouette (every third skinned vertex projected through its bone) inside the same window. `tools/qa-world-ending-client.mjs` captures one live frame per cut window (celebration, six-step, spin, one-claw freeze, landmark, farewell, credits) and renders each captured observation with the baseline commit's code and this tree's code, each with its own camera. `tests/ending-camera.test.mjs` checks the cut mapping over a full controller run and the stage frame for 8, 11 and 18 guests.

Captures and the before/after sheet: `docs/qa/frost-ending-polish-2-before-after.jpg`, originals in `tmp/qa/ending-polish2-final-{desktop,narrow,desktop-high}/`. Self-review and its evidence are in PR #117; root design review owns acceptance.
